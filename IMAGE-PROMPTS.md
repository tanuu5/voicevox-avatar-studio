# 画像生成記録

組み込みimage_genツールで生成しました。アプリで使用する素材は `public/assets/sprites.png` です。外観は非公式の生成イラストです。

## 採用画像のプロンプト

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: animation-ready transparent sprite sheet for a local Zundamon explainer avatar. Draw the recognizable humanoid Zundamon: light green short bob hair with two large edamame ear-like tufts, brown eyes, white blouse and dark green pinafore with bow. Cute clean professional Japanese 2D anime cel illustration. Exact regular grid 3 columns by 2 rows, six equal square cells, identical centered waist-up pose, head size and eye/mouth coordinates in every cell, lots of transparent margin between figures. Cell order reading left to right: neutral eyes open mouth CLOSED, happy eyes curved smiling mouth CLOSED, angry frowning eyes mouth CLOSED, sad teary eyes mouth CLOSED, joyful laughing eyes mouth CLOSED, neutral eyes CLOSED for blink mouth CLOSED. All six characters exactly same position/scale within cell. Flat light peach face skin, no nose shading, mouth tiny at identical location so animated mouth can be overlaid. True transparent background. No text, labels, panels, grid lines, watermark or shadows outside the character.

背景を透過する追加編集も試しましたが、位置の維持を優先し、アプリには最初の生成結果を採用しています。
